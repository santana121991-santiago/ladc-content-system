# 5 Instagram Reel Ideas — LADC Business Integration
**Goal:** Tie sobriety + discipline journey into car detailing. Build trust. Drive bookings.

---

## REEL 1 — "Same Hands"

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
Close-up of your hands holding a detail spray gun.
Text on screen: *"These hands used to shake every morning."*

**Exact shots to film:**
1. Tight shot — hands gripping detail spray gun (close enough to see knuckles)
2. You spraying and wiping a hood in slow motion
3. Before shot of a dirty car, after shot of it gleaming
4. Quick cut — you at the gym, same hands gripping a barbell
5. End shot — you standing next to a finished car, arms crossed, looking at it

**Voiceover:**
> "These hands used to shake every morning from drinking.
> Now they build something.
> Every car I detail is proof that I showed up.
> That's what you're getting when you book LADC.
> Not just a clean car. A guy who doesn't quit."

**Text overlay (for silent viewers):**
- "Shaking from alcohol → Building a business"
- "Same hands. Different life."

**Call to action:**
*"Book your detail in the link in bio. I show up every time."*

## REEL 2 — "While They Were Sleeping"

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
Phone screen showing 5:30 AM timestamp.
Text on screen: *"While most people slept, I was already working."*

**Exact shots to film:**
1. Phone alarm or clock — 5:30 AM
2. You loading your detailing equipment into your car in the dark
3. Driving to a client's house (dashboard cam or passenger angle)
4. Time-lapse or fast cuts of the full detail job
5. Final shot — finished car in early morning light, you giving a thumbs up or walking away

**Voiceover:**
> "A year ago I was up at 5:30 AM too.
> Hungover. Useless. Wasting the day.
> Now I'm up loading equipment and driving to my first job.
> Same time. Completely different man.
> If you want someone who actually shows up — that's me."

**Text overlay:**
- "365 days ago: drunk by midnight, useless by morning"
- "Today: first job booked before 6 AM"

**Call to action:**
*"DM me 'DETAIL' to get a quote. I answer fast."*

## REEL 3 — "The Before and After Nobody Talks About"

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
Split screen. Left: a wrecked, dirty car interior. Right: your face, tired, rough.
Text on screen: *"Two things that needed a complete transformation."*

**Exact shots to film:**
1. Dirty car interior — crumbs, stains, trash (use a real client car or stage one)
2. Old photo of yourself (low point — if you have one, or describe it in text)
3. You working — vacuuming, wiping, detailing with focus and intensity
4. Gym clip — you training hard, same energy
5. Side-by-side final reveal — clean car interior + current physique or clean lifestyle shot

**Voiceover:**
> "People love a good before and after.
> Here's mine.
> The car? I can fix that in a few hours.
> The man? That took everything I had.
> Sobriety. The gym. Building LADC from nothing.
> When you hire me, you're hiring the after."

**Text overlay:**
- "The car: trashed → spotless"
- "The man: lost → locked in"

**Call to action:**
*"Your car deserves the same transformation. Link in bio to book."*

## REEL 4 — "I Started This Business for Them"

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
You on camera, dead serious, looking directly into lens.
*"I didn't start this business to get rich."*

**Exact shots to film:**
1. You talking directly to camera — no filter, natural light, parked car or garage
2. B-roll of your kids (silhouette or from behind if you want to protect their privacy — or show them if you're comfortable)
3. You detailing a car — focused, deliberate, no rushing
4. Close-up of LADC branding on your equipment or shirt
5. Final shot — you and the finished car, or you driving away to the next job

**Voiceover / On-camera script:**
> "I started this business because I needed to become someone my kids could be proud of.
> I was drinking. I was checked out. I wasn't present.
> Getting sober forced me to ask — okay, now what?
> So I started LADC.
> Every job I do is for them.
> That's why I don't cut corners.
> That's why I show up when it's cold, when I'm tired, when it'd be easier not to.
> If you want your car done right — book me."

**Text overlay:**
- "Started for my kids"
- "Built through discipline"
- "LADC — I don't cut corners"

**Call to action:**
*"Link in bio. Book a detail with someone who actually gives a damn."*

## REEL 5 — "What Quitting Alcohol Taught Me About Detailing"

**Hook (0–3 sec):**
Text on screen over close-up detail shot:
*"Sobriety and detailing have one thing in common."*

**Exact shots to film:**
1. Extreme close-up — buffing a scratch out of a panel, slow and deliberate
2. You talking to camera from your car or garage
3. Fast cuts — gym, early morning, detailing, journaling or phone notes
4. Satisfying detail reveal — paint correction, interior clean, wheels shining
5. End frame — you standing in front of a finished car

**Voiceover:**
> "Sobriety taught me one thing above everything else.
> You can't rush the process.
> You can't fake it. You can't skip steps.
> You either do the work every day or you don't.
> That's exactly how I detail cars.
> No shortcuts. No rushing. No half-ass jobs.
> The same discipline that keeps me sober keeps your car looking perfect.
> That's the standard at LADC."

**Text overlay:**
- "No shortcuts in sobriety"
- "No shortcuts in my work"
- "Same standard. Every time."

**Call to action:**
*"DM me or hit the link in bio. First-time clients get a free interior vacuum with any package."*
