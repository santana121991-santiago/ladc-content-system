# 7-Day Instagram Content Plan — LADC
**Goal:** Build trust, grow followers, drive detailing bookings.
**Format:** 1 Reel per day. Simple to film solo. No studio needed.

---

## DAY 1 — MONDAY
**Theme:** Sobriety
**Goal:** Views + new followers

**Hook:**
> *"I was the fun one. Until I wasn't."*

**Reel Idea:**
Talk directly to camera. No script. Tell the moment you knew alcohol was costing you more than it was giving you. One specific moment — not vague. End with where you are now.

**Shots to film:**
- Face on camera, natural light, car or garage background
- Quick b-roll: gym, morning routine, or LADC gear

**Caption hook:** "I didn't quit drinking because I wanted to. I quit because I had nothing left to lose."

---

## DAY 2 — TUESDAY
**Theme:** Discipline
**Goal:** Saves + shares

**Hook:**
> *"Motivation is a lie. I know. I believed it."*

**Reel Idea:**
Show your actual morning. Alarm goes off. You get up. No hype music, no perfect setup. Just what the grind actually looks like — unglamorous and real.

**Shots to film:**
- Phone alarm (real time — 5 or 6 AM)
- You making coffee or getting dressed
- Loading detailing equipment or driving
- End on a finished car or gym clip

**Caption hook:** "Nobody feels like it. That's the whole point."

---

## DAY 3 — WEDNESDAY
**Theme:** Car Detailing (LADC)
**Goal:** Bookings

**Hook:**
> *"I detail cars because I can't drink anymore."*

**Reel Idea:**
Fast-cut transformation video. Dirty car in. Clean car out. You working the whole way through. Add text explaining that the same discipline keeping you sober is what goes into every job.

**Shots to film:**
- Dirty car before (interior or exterior)
- You working — spraying, wiping, vacuuming
- Close-up detail shots (wheels, paint, seats)
- Final reveal — full clean car

**Caption hook:** "Same discipline. Every single car."
**CTA:** "DM me or hit the link in bio to book."

---

## DAY 4 — THURSDAY
**Theme:** Lifestyle / Grind
**Goal:** Trust + relatability

**Hook:**
> *"Stop calling it a grind. You chose this."*

**Reel Idea:**
Day-in-the-life format. Show the full load — job, detailing, gym, family. No complaining. No "it's so hard" energy. Just: this is what building looks like. Own it.

**Shots to film:**
- Morning (alarm, coffee)
- Work or commute
- Detailing job mid-day or after work
- Gym session
- End of night — simple, quiet, no alcohol in sight

**Caption hook:** "Full-time job. Side business. Gym. Sober. This is the life I chose. I wouldn't trade it."

---

## DAY 5 — FRIDAY
**Theme:** Sobriety + Kids
**Goal:** Viral views + shares

**Hook:**
> *"Comfortable men raise soft kids. That scared me."*

**Reel Idea:**
Your most emotional reel of the week. Talk about your kids. What kind of man were you showing them before? What are you showing them now? Keep it short — 30 to 45 seconds max. Let it breathe. Don't rush it.

**Shots to film:**
- You on camera, serious and direct
- Optional: silhouette of kids, or shots from behind (protect their privacy)
- B-roll: you working, at the gym, or early morning

**Caption hook:** "I used to show my kids what giving up looks like. Now I show them what showing up looks like."

---

## DAY 6 — SATURDAY
**Theme:** Car Detailing (LADC)
**Goal:** Bookings + local reach

**Hook:**
> *"6 AM. No excuses. A car to clean."*

**Reel Idea:**
Behind the scenes of a real job. Show up early. Film the process from arrival to reveal. Add text: what you charge, what's included, how to book. Make it feel professional but personal.

**Shots to film:**
- Arriving at client's location early
- Unloading your equipment
- Full detail process (time-lapse works great here)
- Final walk-around of the finished car
- You shaking hands with the client or giving a thumbs up

**Caption hook:** "This is what a $[your price] detail looks like. Worth every cent."
**CTA:** "Link in bio to book your spot. Limited availability."

---

## DAY 7 — SUNDAY
**Theme:** Discipline / Reflection
**Goal:** Follows + community trust

**Hook:**
> *"Most men won't change until they lose everything."*

**Reel Idea:**
Weekly reflection. What did this week teach you? What did you do that your old self never would have? Keep it raw. This one builds the deepest loyalty — people come back for this energy every week.

**Shots to film:**
- You talking to camera — end of day, relaxed, honest
- B-roll from the week (recycle clips you already filmed)
- Optional: journal, morning coffee, quiet moment

**Caption hook:** "Week [X] done. Sober. Building. Not stopping."

## Filming Rules for the Week

1. **Film in batches.** Shoot Monday + Tuesday content on Sunday night. Shoot Wednesday + Thursday on Tuesday. You need 2-3 hours total, not 7 separate sessions.
2. **Natural light only.** No ring lights. Raw lighting builds trust in your niche.
3. **Film more than you need.** Capture 3x what you think you'll use. Edit down from there.
4. **One take is fine.** Perfect delivery kills authenticity. Your audience wants real.
5. **Save every clip.** Unused footage becomes b-roll for future weeks.
